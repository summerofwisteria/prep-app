Privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Who this app is for

Prep is a personal application used by one person and a small number of people she knows personally. It is not available to the public.

What data Prep accesses

With her permission, Prep reads the following from her Google Health account, using read-only access: activity and fitness data (such as steps and workouts), health metrics and measurements (such as heart rate, heart rate variability, and weight), sleep data, and nutrition logs. Prep cannot write, change, or delete any Google Health data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to calculate and display personal trends and summaries for her and the people she has chosen to share them with. It is not used for advertising, is never sold, and is not used to build profiles for anyone else.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored on a personal computer controlled by the app's owner. It is not stored on public servers. Remote access is only available over a private, encrypted network.

Sharing with third parties

To produce written weekly summaries, Prep sends aggregated figures (such as weekly averages and trends), not raw records, to Anthropic's Claude API. No other third party receives her data. Prep's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

She can ask for all stored data to be deleted at any time, and it will be deleted from the app's storage and backups. She can also revoke Prep's access at any time from her Google Account settings, under third-party connections.
